SDE Skills is a volunteer-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit that helps software engineers prepare for technical interviews through free weekly system design, data structures and algorithms, and LeetCode practice sessions.

SDE Skills is an organization with the singular vision to bridge the gap between the skills job seekers possess and the skills that they need to succeed. We provide the resources and support necessary to maintain the sharpness of their technical skills.
