Data Access:

We store your email address and name on our marketing list and use it to contact you on a regular basis. We do not share or sell your data.

Advertising Measurement:

This site does not show third-party ads and we do not sell data. We promote our free programs with Google Ads (including Google Ad Grants) and Meta, and we use the Google Ads tag and the Meta Pixel to measure whether those ads lead people to our programs, for example when someone clicks to join our Discord community. These tools may use cookies and collect device and usage data under Google's and Meta's own privacy policies. You can limit this through your browser settings, Google Ad Settings or Meta ad preferences.

Analytics:

We use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use this site (for example, which pages are viewed). Google may process your IP address and device data to provide this service.
